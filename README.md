# Multimedia Project - AI-Generated Video Explanation on General Medical Literacy

A multimedia educational series created using AI to spread general health awareness by making complex medical information accessible to everyone.

## Project Objective
To bridge the health literacy gap by creating a series of easy-to-understand audio-visual episodes. The project transforms trusted resources—specifically, official fact sheet PDFs from the World Health Organization (WHO) website—into engaging explainer videos using Google's NotebookLM.

## How It Works
* **Trusted Sources:** The project strictly uses verified PDFs directly from the WHO website to ensure 100% medical accuracy.
* **AI Generation:** NotebookLM is used to synthesize these dense documents and generate natural, conversational audio scripts and visual structures.
* **Multimedia Output:** The final result is a series of short, plain-language video episodes designed for a non-clinical general audience.

## Episode List
1. **Air Pollution:** Respiratory health and environmental impacts.
2. **Diabetes:** Type 1 vs. Type 2 and metabolic health mechanisms.
3. **Mental Health:** Workplace stress and psychosocial well-being.
4. **Food Safety:** Biological hazards and everyday safe food handling.
5. **Physical Activity:** Movement as medicine and combating sedentary lifestyles.

## Key Features
* **Trusted Information:** By grounding all AI generation exclusively in WHO PDFs, the project avoids AI hallucinations and spreads only verified health facts.
* **Accessibility-First:** The episodes use simple terminology and clear explanations designed specifically for viewers with low medical literacy.
* **Efficient Content Creation:** Using NotebookLM allows for the rapid creation of educational multimedia content without the need for traditional, resource-intensive video editing.
